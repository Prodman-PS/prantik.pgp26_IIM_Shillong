IBD Digital Twin — system architecture

Five layers in one Python repo. Data flows one way: each layer reads only the layer above. Ground truth is used for labels and evaluation only.
1

Synthetic patients & data streams src/generator/ → data/synthetic/

800 virtual Indian IBD patients × 365 days, seeded (seed 42). Latent inflammation drives every stream with lags, noise, decoys (colds, stress, NSAIDs, infections) and missing days.

EHR: demographics, Montreal class, ICD-10, NOD2 / IL23R / HLAWearables: HRV, RHR, steps, SpO₂, sleep, skin temp, resp rateDaily symptom check-in (partial Mayo / HBI)Labs: calprotectin, CRP, Hb, ferritin…Sweat sensor (investigational)

Fusion & personal baselines src/features/

One row per patient-day built only from data ≤ day t (tested). Each signal is compared with the patient's own robust baseline (median / MAD over observably quiet days).

3

Prediction, explanation & what-if src/models/

Will a flare start in the next 14 days? Logistic baseline vs LightGBM, 5-fold cross-validation grouped by patient, Platt-calibrated, alert threshold chosen on validation patients.

4

Alerts & safety rules src/alerts/

Two tiers. Trend alerts over days to weeks; emergency patterns within hours. A rule-based Cancer Safety panel: surveillance support, not cancer detection.

🟠 Risk ≥ 20% → home calprotectin🔴 Risk ≥ 50% → teleconsult🚨 6 emergency rulesCRC risk level, colonoscopy due date, red flags

5

Doctor dashboard src/dashboard/app.py (Streamlit)

Population view sorted by risk, with emergency and overdue-surveillance lists. Patient view: card, risk gauge & trend, signals vs baseline band, labs, "why", what-if, suggested action, alerts, Cancer Safety, sweat (investigational). A date slider replays any day.

Ground truth — never a model input

events.csv (flares, emergencies, decoys), ground_truth/latent_daily (labels, latent inflammation), hidden_params.csv.

Used only to build the label flare_within_14d and to evaluate. Lab reason / setting columns are dropped. Only prescriptions and logged colds / infections are read from events.csv as context.

Traffic light

Low < 20%
Rising 20–50%
High ≥ 50%

Emergency rules

  • Acute severe colitis
  • VTE / PE
  • Sepsis on steroids
  • Bowel obstruction
  • Stoma dehydration
  • TB reactivation on anti-TNF

Design choices

  • Disease-agnostic layers 2–5 (COPD, heart failure next)
  • Sensor-agnostic: new streams are new columns
  • Out-of-fold scoring in the UI
